**GeoRelight** is an ongoing research project on controllable video relighting.
The project investigates how text-based lighting instructions and geometric cues,
including depth and surface normals, can guide temporally coherent lighting edits
across video frames.

**Research topics:** video relighting · diffusion transformers · geometric conditioning · temporal consistency

*This is an ongoing research project. Add a public demo, paper, or project video only when it is ready for release.*
