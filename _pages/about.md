I am an AI researcher and computer vision team lead at the **AI Center, Transsion Holdings**.
My work focuses on generative visual intelligence, particularly diffusion-based image and
video generation, visual editing, and efficient deployment-oriented vision technologies.

My research interests include **generative image editing**, **video generation and editing**,
**diffusion models**, and **efficient visual intelligence**. I am interested in developing
robust visual generation and editing systems that connect advances in generative modeling
with practical imaging and content-creation experiences.

Previously, I worked in research and engineering roles at Zhejiang University, Northwestern
Polytechnical University, Ant Group, and OPPO. I received my Ph.D. in Control Science and
Engineering from Tongji University and was a visiting Ph.D. researcher at Michigan State
University.

### Research Interests

- Generative image editing and diffusion models
- Video generation, editing, and relighting
- Visual understanding and intelligent editing
- Efficient generative models for mobile and edge devices
